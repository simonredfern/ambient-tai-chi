If you’ve ever thought about trying Tai Chi or you'd like to try it in a different way, try an Ambient Tai Chi session!

      
Ambient Tai Chi is Live Ambient music plus a relaxed introduction to Tai Chi (Taijiquan), Qi Gong and Pushing Hands (Tui Shou).

      
Stillness, body - mind awareness, and light partner contact to the martial art side of things.

      
Simon Redfern has long practiced Tai Chi and is currently a student of Wolf and Anika at the Ma Tsun Kuen Tai Chi Chuan. Mitch is a long time ambient musician and creator of electronic instruments. We're looking forward to welcoming you!

      
Location: TESOBE GmbH, Osloer Strasse 16/17, 13359 Berlin.
