Ambient Tai Chi is an introduction to Tai Chi (Taijiquan), Qi Gong and Pushing Hands (Tui Shou) to the sound of live ambient music.

      
Stillness, body - mind awareness, and light partner contact for a very basic intro into the martial arts side of things.

      
Simon Redfern has long practiced Tai Chi (originally Yang style in London) and is a programmer, entrepreneur and improvising musician. He's currently a student of Wolf Scheidt and Annika Rinn at the Ma Tsun Kuen Tai Chi Chuan school in Prenzlauer Berg, Belin; and he's happy to introduce Tai Chi to others.

      
Mitch Altman is a Berlin based hacker, electronics engineer and ambient musician. He regularly speaks at hacker conferences and teaches electronics.

      
Next event: 22 August, 13:30–14:15 at the Haus Neudorf Sommerfest, Haus Neudorf e.V., Ort Neudorf 9, 17268 Gerswalde.
