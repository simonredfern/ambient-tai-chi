Ambient Tai Chi is an introduction to Tai Chi (Taijiquan), Qi Gong and Pushing Hands (Tui Shou) to the sound of live ambient music.

      
Stillness, body - mind awareness, and light partner contact for a very basic intro the martial art side of things.

      
Simon Redfern has long practiced Tai Chi and is a programmer, entrepreneur and improvising musician. He's currently a student of Wolf Scheidt and Annika Rinn at the Ma Tsun Kuen Tai Chi Chuan school in Prenzlauer Berg. Mitch Altman is a berlin based hacker, electronics engineer and ambient musician. He regularly speaks at hacker conferences and teaches electronics.

      
Location: TESOBE GmbH, Osloer Strasse 16/17, 13359 Berlin or at a festival near you!
